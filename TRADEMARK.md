# Trademark Policy

The HelpOS **code** is open source under AGPL-3.0. The HelpOS **name and logo** are not licensed under the AGPL. They identify the official project and its hosted service.

## You may

- Say your product **works with**, **integrates with** or is **built on** HelpOS.
- Use the name to refer to the project accurately, for example in articles, tutorials and talks.
- Self-host unmodified or modified HelpOS for your own organisation, keeping the name inside your instance.

## Please ask first before you

- Offer a hosted or commercial service that uses "HelpOS" in its name or branding.
- Distribute a **modified** version publicly under the HelpOS name. Rename your fork instead.
- Use the logo in a way that suggests we endorse you.

## Why

This keeps users from confusing an unofficial fork or hosted service with the official project. It does not limit your rights under the AGPL to use, modify and share the code.
